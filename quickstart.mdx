## Getting Started in 3 Steps

### Step 1: Access Your Navigator

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion icon="globe" title="Use the Demo (Recommended)">
    The fastest way to get started is with our live demo at [app.keinsaas.com](https://app.keinsaas.com).
    
    - No setup required
    - Full feature access
    - Perfect for testing and evaluation
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion icon="cloud" title="Deploy Your Own Instance">
    For production use or custom configurations, you can deploy your own instance:
    
    1. Fork the [navigator repository](https://github.com/cgoinglove/navigator)
    2. Deploy to Vercel, Docker, or your preferred platform
    3. Configure your environment variables
    4. Start using your personalized instance
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>

### Step 2: Configure Your AI Provider

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion icon="key" title="Add Your API Keys">
    Keinsaas Navigator supports multiple AI providers. Choose one or more:
    
    **OpenAI (Recommended)**
    - Get your API key from [OpenAI Platform](https://platform.openai.com/api-keys)
    - Add it to your environment configuration
    
    **Anthropic Claude**
    - Obtain your API key from [Anthropic Console](https://console.anthropic.com/)
    - Configure in your settings
    
    **Google Gemini**
    - Get your API key from [Google AI Studio](https://makersuite.google.com/app/apikey)
    - Add to your configuration
    
    <Tip>You only need one provider to get started, but multiple providers give you more options and redundancy.</Tip>
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion icon="cog" title="Optional: Configure Additional Tools">
    For enhanced functionality, you can optionally configure:
    
    - **Exa AI**: For advanced web search capabilities
    - **Database**: For conversation history and user management
    - **File Storage**: For document uploads and management
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>

### Step 3: Start Using Your Navigator

<Accordion icon="rocket" title="Begin Your First Conversation">
  You're ready to start! Here's how to make the most of your Keinsaas Navigator:
  
  1. **Start a conversation**: Type your question or request
  2. **Use @ mentions**: Type @ to access tools and presets quickly
  3. **Try voice input**: Click the microphone icon for voice interaction
  4. **Explore tools**: Use the tool panel to discover available capabilities
  5. **Customize settings**: Access preferences to personalize your experience
</Accordion>
