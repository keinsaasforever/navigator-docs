## Getting Started with Voice

### Basic Voice Commands

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion icon="play" title="Starting a Voice Session">
    **To start voice interaction:**
    
    1. Click the microphone icon in the chat interface
    2. Grant microphone permissions when prompted
    3. Start speaking naturally
    4. The assistant will respond with both voice and text
    
    **Voice activation phrases:**
    - "Hey Navigator, help me with..."
    - "I need to..."
    - "Can you..."
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion icon="stop" title="Controlling Voice Sessions">
    **To control voice interaction:**
    
    - **Pause**: Say "pause" or click the pause button
    - **Resume**: Say "continue" or click the resume button
    - **Stop**: Say "stop" or click the stop button
    - **Interrupt**: Simply start speaking to interrupt the assistant
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>

## Voice Quality and Performance

### Optimizing Voice Recognition

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion icon="microphone" title="Microphone Setup">
    **For best results:**
    
    - Use a high-quality microphone or headset
    - Minimize background noise
    - Speak clearly and at normal pace
    - Position microphone 6-12 inches from your mouth
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion icon="wifi" title="Network Requirements">
    **Internet connection:**
    
    - Stable broadband connection recommended
    - Minimum 1 Mbps upload speed
    - Low latency for real-time processing
    - Consider wired connection for best performance
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion icon="cog" title="Voice Settings">
    **Customize your experience:**
    
    - Adjust speech recognition sensitivity
    - Set preferred language and accent
    - Configure voice response speed
    - Choose voice output preferences
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>

## Best Practices

### Voice Interaction Tips
