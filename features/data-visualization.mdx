### Data Quality

- **Clean Your Data**: Ensure data is accurate, complete, and properly formatted
- **Validate Results**: Double-check calculations and verify data relationships
- **Document Sources**: Keep track of data sources and any transformations applied
- **Test Visualizations**: Verify that visualizations accurately represent the underlying data
- **Regular Updates**: Keep visualizations current with fresh data
